Frequently Asked Questions

What areas of pharmaceutical consultancy do you offer?

I offer professional guidance in Quality Control laboratory management, GMP compliance, analytical method validation (HPLC, UV, GC, AAS, FTIR), OOS/OOT investigation, CAPA implementation, data integrity (ALCOA+), WHO-GMP and ICH guideline implementation, and stability study planning. I also provide in-house training support for QC personnel.

Are you available for collaborations on research publications?

Yes, I welcome research collaboration opportunities in pharmaceutical sciences, quality control, pharmacology, and related fields. I have authored 3 publications in international peer-reviewed journals with impact factors of 7.4–7.5. Please email me with your research proposal and we can discuss the scope of collaboration.

Do you provide GMP audit support for pharmaceutical companies in Nepal?

With over 10 years of experience in GMP-compliant environments and thorough knowledge of WHO-GMP, DDA Nepal regulations, ASEAN GMP, and USFDA guidelines, I can provide professional support for GMP audit preparation, documentation review, and compliance gap analysis. Please contact me to discuss your specific requirements.

What languages can you conduct training or communication in?

I am fluent in English, Nepali, Hindi, Bengali, and Bhojpuri — both written and spoken. I can conduct training sessions and professional communications in any of these languages, making me accessible to pharmaceutical professionals across Nepal, India, and Bangladesh.

How can I access your published research papers?

My published papers are indexed in the International Journal of Pharmaceutical Research and Applications and International Journal in Pharmaceutical Sciences. You can access them via the DOI links provided on the About Author page, or contact me directly and I will share available copies as permissible under the journal's open access policy.
