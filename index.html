About Pradeep Kumar

Pradeep Kumar is a highly experienced pharmaceutical professional currently serving as QC Manager at Zydin Biotech Ltd., Saranpur, Bharatpur-16, Chitwan, Nepal, where he oversees Oncology API & Formulations at the R&D stage. He brings over a decade of specialized expertise in Quality Control laboratory management, GMP/GLP compliance, and pharmaceutical regulatory affairs.

With a Bachelor in Pharmacy (Hons) from Bangladesh University, Dhaka, Pradeep has built a distinguished career across leading pharmaceutical companies in Nepal, including Royal Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. and Magnus Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. His expertise spans across Oral Solid Dosages (OSDs), external preparations, oral liquids, and oncology formulations.

He is well-versed in an extensive range of analytical instruments including HPLC (Shimadzu, Agilent, Waters, PerkinElmer), UV Spectrophotometry, Gas Chromatography, AAS, FTIR, and more. His regulatory knowledge encompasses WHO-GMP, USFDA, ICH Guidelines, ASEAN, DDA, Revised Schedule M, and USP General Chapters. Pradeep is also a published researcher with three papers in international peer-reviewed journals with an impact factor of 7.4–7.5.
